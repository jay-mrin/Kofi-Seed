About Us

A personalised writing platform.

Seed Garden is a personalised digital-writing platform. Custom writings are prepared from the buyer’s request and delivered electronically by email.

Seed Garden offers personalised digital writing created from the request, prayer, intention, or message you submit. Each custom order is prepared with care and attention to the details you share, then delivered electronically.

How It Works

  1. Choose a personalised writing order.
  2. Enter the email address where your writing should be delivered. Your name and message are optional.
  3. Complete payment securely through PayPal.
  4. We prepare the personalised writing and deliver it electronically.
